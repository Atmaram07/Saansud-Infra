GOOD TO KNOW

Questions worth
asking early.

Clear expectations help make better project decisions.

What information should I share first?

Project type, location, approximate area, intended scope, budget expectations and preferred timeline are useful starting points.

Can I request an estimate before a site visit?

You can share your initial requirements. The level of detail possible depends on the drawings, specifications and site information available.

Are timelines and costs fixed from the beginning?

A reliable proposal should state its scope, assumptions, exclusions and dependencies. Changes or unforeseen site conditions may affect cost and schedule under the agreed contract.

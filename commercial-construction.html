GOOD TO KNOW

Questions worth
asking early.

Clear expectations help make better project decisions.

What details are needed to discuss a commercial project?

Share the intended business use, site location, approximate area, operating constraints, available plans, desired opening date and any known building requirements.

Can work be phased around business operations?

Phasing depends on the site, safety needs, access and work scope. Discuss operating hours, restricted areas and handover stages during project planning.

Who confirms approvals and technical requirements?

Responsibilities vary by project. Confirm the required consultants, submissions, approvals and sign-offs with the relevant professionals and local authorities before work begins.

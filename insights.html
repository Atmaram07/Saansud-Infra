BHUBANESWAR PROJECT GUIDES

Plan the questions
before you build.

Use these checklists to prepare for a construction or interiors discussion. Site-specific technical and approval questions should be confirmed with qualified local professionals.

01 / ESTIMATES

Compare construction estimates by scope

Check that each estimate uses the same drawings, built-up area and specification level. Ask for inclusions and exclusions, structural and finishing assumptions, services, taxes, debris removal, site access assumptions and the process for approving variations. A low headline rate is difficult to compare when the scope differs.

02 / SITE PLANNING

Prepare for a site assessment

Share the plot location, available drawings, intended number of floors, access constraints and your preferred timeline. Ask which surveys, soil information, structural inputs and local approvals may be needed for your particular site. Requirements depend on the property and should be checked with the relevant professionals and authorities.

03 / INTERIORS

Set an interior budget that is comparable

List the rooms and items in scope, then separate civil changes, electrical and lighting, fixed furniture, loose furniture, appliances and finishes. Confirm material brands or quality levels, measurement assumptions, delivery and installation responsibilities, and what is excluded before comparing proposals.

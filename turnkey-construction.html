GOOD TO KNOW

Questions worth
asking early.

Clear expectations help make better project decisions.

What does turnkey construction include?

The term can cover different scopes. Ask for a written list of included work, material specifications, design responsibilities, exclusions, procurement, approvals, handover items and warranty terms.

How can I compare turnkey quotations?

Compare the same drawings and specifications, payment milestones, assumed quantities, taxes, exclusions and variation process. Ask how changes are priced and approved.

Can I choose materials during execution?

Agree how selections are documented, which substitutions require approval and how a change may affect cost or schedule before signing the project scope.

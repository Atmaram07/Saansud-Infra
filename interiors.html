GOOD TO KNOW

Questions worth
asking early.

Clear expectations help make better project decisions.

What should an interior design and execution brief include?

List the rooms, how each space will be used, storage needs, style preferences, appliances, existing items to retain and the budget range. Share plans and site measurements when available.

What affects an interior project budget?

Room count, built-in furniture, materials, hardware, lighting, electrical changes, appliances, site measurements and installation scope all affect the proposal. Check what is excluded.

How do I compare interior quotations?

Check itemized quantities, material brands or equivalent quality descriptions, drawings, finish samples, installation responsibilities, taxes and the process for approving changes.
